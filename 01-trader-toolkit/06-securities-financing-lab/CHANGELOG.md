# Changelog

All notable changes to the Equity Borrow & Financing Scenario Lab are recorded
here. The project follows semantic versioning.

## [1.0.0] — 2026-08-10

### Added

- Interactive short-sale and stock-borrow scenario controls.
- Auditable price P&L, rebate, borrow-fee, execution-cost and recall-cost legs.
- Borrow-adjusted break-even decline and locate-coverage measures.
- Price-move versus borrow-fee sensitivity heatmap.
- Illustrative P&L path and line-by-line trade-economics table.
- Utilization, locate availability and recall-risk interpretation.
- Fictional sample inventory data and five analytics tests.
- Dark navy and teal Streamlit interface, dashboard previews and Mac launcher.
- Research framing, workflow diagrams, limitations and reproducibility notes.
