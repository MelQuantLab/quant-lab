# Corporate Actions, Borrow & Relative-Value Monitor

> Work in progress — project development started 22 August 2026.

An event-driven research tool for monitoring corporate actions, earnings and other market catalysts that may affect equity prices, stock-borrow availability and financing economics.

The planned workflow will turn a noisy event feed into an auditable desk view with:

- sector-classified corporate actions and earnings;
- potential borrow-demand, utilization and fee-pressure flags;
- seven-day and one-month catalyst horizons;
- relative-value, pair-trading and event-arbitrage candidates;
- explicit liquidity, crowding, recall and downside-risk controls; and
- simple outputs that a graduate analyst can use and a senior trader can audit.

The working Streamlit prototype, analytics module, sample event data and tests will be reviewed and added in the next development session.
